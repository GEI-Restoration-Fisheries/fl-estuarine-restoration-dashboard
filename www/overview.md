## Welcome to the Dashboard!

The **Florida Estuarine Restoration Database** contains information about thousands of habitat restoration projects spanning multiple decades across Florida’s estuarine ecosystems, compiled and harmonized from several open-access data sources. Use this dashboard to explore the database in real time, customize the data to meet your specific interests or needs, download the data you want, and contribute your own habitat restoration projects to the database. The database is a product of the Gulf Ecosystem Initiative's <a href="https://www.nceas.ucsb.edu/workinggroups/gei-review-impact-large-scale-estuarine-habitat-restoration-fisheries-gulf-mexico" target="_blank">Restoration & Fisheries Working Group</a> and maintained by the Tampa Bay Estuary Program.
